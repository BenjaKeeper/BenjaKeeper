## What I work on

- **AI agents in production.** An agent that books medical appointments over WhatsApp, phone, Instagram, Messenger, email and web chat ([SuiteMedica](https://bcristi.com/en/work/suitemedica-ai-agent-clinics/)).
- **Healthcare operations.** Haemodynamics operating-room management: inventory, usage logging, analytics and costs ([HemoSuite / OR360](https://bcristi.com/en/work/hemosuite-haemodynamics-operating-room-back-office/)); clinic finance and analytics ([ICV OS](https://bcristi.com/en/work/icv-os-clinic-finance-platform/)).
- **AI on top of systems you can't replace.** A dashboard and AI module over an ERP that could not be modified ([CARS Control](https://bcristi.com/en/work/cars-control-automotive-erp/)).
- **Traceability.** A mining inspection lab, from sample intake to certificate ([Alex Stewart](https://bcristi.com/en/work/alex-stewart-molybdenum-traceability/)).
- **Access control for residential communities.** [Keeper](https://bcristi.com/en/work/keeper-access-control-communities/): 2,500 active users and 1.53 million logged visits.

At Blanco Brand I run a platform of 25 deployed projects with a stable team of three: deployments went from one hour to five minutes, uptime is 99.95% and development spend is down 63%.

Before that: Tech Lead at Conomy (fintech, Flutter; Startup Chile Build 3, shortlisted at the 11:FS Awards 2024) and Mobile Tech Lead at Komatsu Chile.
